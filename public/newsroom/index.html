entomo Labs signs agreement to acquire the Asia, Middle East, and Africa (AMEA) operations of Dedalus, strengthening its digital health offering

The agreement for this strategic acquisition comes at a pivotal time, as countries across the region ramp up efforts to build Digital Public Infrastructure for Health (DPI-H) — focusing on open standards, patient-centric care, and interoperable platforms. By joining forces, entomo Labs and Dedalus AMEA will create a unified, future-ready offering that serves both public health and provider health networks.

About adeahub

adeahub is a digital health platform that helps hospitals, health systems, and national health authorities deliver personalised care at scale. Its four solutions — AH Engage, AH Care, AH Connect, and AH Insights — work together to unify clinical workflows, connect disparate systems, and surface the data clinicians need to act. With 8,500+ healthcare facilities and more than 200 million patient records across its installed base, and over 25 years of deployment experience in Asia, the Middle East, and Africa, adeahub powers modern healthcare where it is needed most. Learn more at adeahub.co.
