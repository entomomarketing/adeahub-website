The challenge

When COVID-19 arrived, health systems had no unified way to detect cases early, trace contacts, and monitor patients at population scale. Manual processes could not keep pace, and hospitals filled with cases that could have been managed at home.

What we did

adeahub deployed a national outbreak management platform for the Ministry of Health — unifying disease surveillance, contact tracing, remote monitoring, vaccination, and risk communication in a single system that scaled from clinics to the whole population.

The vision

See the outbreak before it spreads.

Disease outbreaks are inevitable. The damage they cause is not. When detection is late and the response is fragmented, hospitals are overwhelmed and lives are lost that should not have been.

AH Outbreak Management gives public health teams one platform to see risk early and act on it — uniting live surveillance, contact tracing, remote monitoring, and response playbooks in a single coordinated system.

Teams move from reacting to the headlines to acting ahead of them, containing outbreaks while protecting the people and operations that depend on them.

The end-to-end workflow

Manage a disease outbreak at scale.

Six coordinated steps take public health teams from nationwide readiness to post-release recovery — each one builds on the last.

1

Outbreak Management (Nation-wide)

Register the whole population, map cases by area, and push geo-targeted alerts across every zone and district.

2

Quarantine

Manage positive cases under digital Home Surveillance Orders, with daily health checks and automatic ER escalation.

3

Contact Tracing

Identify close contacts and place them under surveillance orders to limit movement and get tested.

4

Access Management

QR check-in at public venues keeps infected and exposed people out of high-risk spaces.

5

Border Surveillance

Screen every arrival by infection status, with digital arrival cards and risk-based entry at borders.

6

Post Release

Vaccination management, lab and LIS integration, and nationwide testing close the loop after recovery.

What it covers

Every lever you need to contain an outbreak.

One platform spans detection, control and recovery — the full health-emergency readiness toolkit, plus the public app citizens use every day.

Who it’s for

Built for the people who manage outbreaks.

AH Outbreak Management gives public health, clinical, and technology leaders the outcomes each role depends on when every hour counts.

Ministry · Disease Control Officer

For Public Health Leaders

Triage the population by risk and act on it. Live surveillance, contact tracing, and public dashboards turn fragmented signals into coordinated national response. Legally backed home surveillance orders and risk-based border controls give your teams the levers to contain spread early.

CMO · CMIO · Hospital Director

For Clinical Leaders

Protect your facility with isolation-zone monitoring, SOP playbooks, and resource tracking that prepare staff before a surge arrives. Remote monitoring keeps low-severity cases out of your beds, so clinical teams focus on the patients who need them most.

CIO · CTO · VP Applications

For IT Leaders

Deploy the core in your first phase without heavy integration, then scale as maturity grows. Open HL7 and FHIR R4 standards keep you connected to existing systems and national registries. WHO GDHCN compatibility and Zero Trust security let your team launch fast and stay compliant. Runs on cloud or on-premise.
