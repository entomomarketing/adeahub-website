Many crafts, one team

Taking a hospital or a national health programme live is never one person's work. Product, engineering, delivery, support and commercial teams plan it together, build it together and stay with it long after go-live.

Whatever your craft, you will work closely with people from the others. These are the areas our teams work in.

Engineering

Build and run the platform and its products, from the clinical workstation in AH Care to health data exchange in AH Connect.

Product

Decide what gets built and why, working with clinicians, administrators and the teams who implement it.

Delivery and implementation

Take hospitals and national programmes live: solution design, integration, training and go-live.

Support services

Keep live systems dependable for the clinicians and administrators who use them every day.

Commercial

Work with hospitals, health systems and ministries of health across our regions.

Finance, legal and marketing

The functions that keep a company with 17+ locations running well.

What we stand for

Five values shape how we work with each other and with the health systems we serve. If they sound like how you want to work, you will feel at home here.

Patient First
Every product decision starts with one question: does this make care better for the patient at the end of the journey?
Evidence Over Hype
We measure success in clinical outcomes and operational results, not capability lists or feature announcements.
Open by Design
We build on open standards so health data can flow freely and securely to where patients and providers need it most.
Clinically Rigorous
Our workflows are built to clinical standards, validated in real health systems, and trusted by clinicians who use them daily.
Partners, Not Vendors
We invest in long-term relationships with the health systems we serve, and we stay long after go-live.
