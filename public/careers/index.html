Work that ends up in front of patients and clinicians

We are a team of healthcare technologists and clinicians. What we build is specific, closely regulated and used every day, and that is what makes it worth doing.

It reaches people

The platform serves 35M+ users and holds 200M+ patient records. The software our teams ship is used in clinics, hospitals and national health programmes.

The customers are health systems

We work with ministries of health and hospital groups across 8,500+ health facilities. Their problems are specific and consequential, and they shape what we build.

The team sits close to care

With 17+ locations across Asia Pacific, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas, our delivery, commercial and engineering teams work near the health systems they serve.

It builds on 25+ years

adeahub stands on a healthcare IT lineage that goes back to 1998. You work alongside people who have implemented hospital systems for decades.

What we stand for

Five values shape how we build, implement and support the platform. If they sound like how you want to work, you will feel at home here.

Patient First
Every product decision starts with one question: does this make care better for the patient at the end of the journey?
Evidence Over Hype
We measure success in clinical outcomes and operational results, not capability lists or feature announcements.
Open by Design
We build on open standards so health data can flow freely and securely to where patients and providers need it most.
Clinically Rigorous
Our workflows are built to clinical standards, validated in real health systems, and trusted by clinicians who use them daily.
Partners, Not Vendors
We invest in long-term relationships with the health systems we serve, and we stay long after go-live.
