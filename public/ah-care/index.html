Case Study

Mouwasat Medical Services

Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — Six-Hospital Network

HIMSS EMRAM Stage 7

The challenge

Mouwasat Medical Services operates six acute-care hospitals across Saudi Arabia — in Dammam, Khobar, Jubail, and Madinah — with beds ranging from 120 to 280 per facility. Managing clinical workflows, patient records, pharmacy, labs, and billing consistently across all six sites, while meeting the world’s highest digital health benchmarks, demanded a single, deeply integrated clinical platform.

What we did

adeahub deployed AH Care across all six Mouwasat hospitals, unifying 30+ clinical modules — from patient administration and EMR through to pharmacy, laboratory, radiology, and billing — on a single integrated platform. The deployment was designed from the outset to meet HIMSS EMRAM Stage 7 requirements at every site, providing closed-loop medication management, evidence-based clinical decision support, and full analytics capability.

Multi-facilities, Multi-domains, Multi-Disciplines. One Integrated System.

AH Care covers every function from patient arrival to discharge — modular by design, unified in delivery.

Patient Administration

Registration, scheduling, bed management, and patient flow — integrated with billing and insurance to eliminate duplicate data entry at every touchpoint.

EMR & CPOE

Structured clinical documentation, physician order entry, clinical decision support, and closed-loop medication administration — the foundation of HIMSS EMRAM Stage 7.

Billing & Revenue Cycle Management

AH Care closes the revenue loop from patient registration to final settlement. Automated insurance pre-authorisation, structured claim generation, and direct claim submission reduce manual effort at every step. Built-in denial management flags rejected claims with reason codes and routes them for correction, while exception handling ensures no billable encounter falls through the gap — protecting revenue and accelerating reimbursements.

Laboratory & Radiology

Order-to-result workflows for laboratory and radiology — results auto-posted to the clinical record the moment they are verified, with critical value alerting to the responsible clinician.

Emergency (ER) & Operation Theatre (OR)

Dedicated ER workflows for triage, rapid assessment, and patient handoff — combined with full surgical scheduling, anaesthesia records, and intraoperative documentation for the OR.

Pharmacy & Supply Chain

Closed-loop medication management from order through dispensing — with barcode verification and drug interaction alerts. Centralised inventory tracks consumption and manages par levels across your entire facility network.

Any Device. Anywhere. Any Time.

AH Care is built on an open architecture — accessible from any browser, on any device, from any location. No proprietary hardware lock-in. Deploy on cloud, on-premise, or hybrid infrastructure, and connect seamlessly to existing health systems via open standards.

Intelligence built into every clinical workflow

adea AI runs inside AH Care — not bolted on top. It surfaces the right guidance at the right moment, so clinicians act sooner and patients stay safer.

Clinical Decision Support

Point of care

Evidence-based alerts and order-set recommendations surface at the point of care, inside the physician’s existing workflow. Drug interaction warnings, dosing checks, and protocol guidance trigger in real time — reducing clinical errors without adding documentation burden.

Automated Triage & Alerts

Emergency Department

Intelligent triage scoring in the ER prioritises patients by acuity and flags deteriorating cases to the responsible team in real time. Critical lab values and vital sign anomalies trigger immediate alerts — no manual monitoring required.

Intelligent Documentation with AI Scribe

EMR & coding

AI-assisted documentation cuts the time clinicians spend on note entry — auto-populating structured fields from prior encounters, suggesting ICD codes from free text, and flagging gaps before discharge to support accurate coding.

Claims & Denial Intelligence

Revenue cycle

adea AI monitors claim submissions in real time, predicting denial risk before submission and routing high-risk claims for review. Denial patterns are analysed across payers to surface systemic issues — reducing rejections and protecting collections at scale.
