The platform

Enterprise-grade. Open standards. Built to last.

adeahub is a unified digital health platform built on open standards — FHIR, HL7, ICD, and REST APIs.

  • Scales from a primary health centre to a national EHR
  • Centralised, decentralised, and hybrid deployments across cloud and on-premise
  • Integrates with existing EHRs, national health stacks, devices, and third-party systems — without replacing them
  • Built-in Zero Trust security and data governance
  • Active half-yearly release cycle
  • Trusted by 35M+ users worldwide
